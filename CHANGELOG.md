## 1.0.0

- Initial release. Eight biome tiers from Meadows to the Deep North.
- Every pack needs Protection Idols of the matching tier: 1 to craft, 2 for 2 stars, 3 for 3 stars.
- Teleport is on for every pack by default. The README explains how to turn it off.
- Cinder Pack is crafted at Black Forge level 3, the same level as Flametal armor.
